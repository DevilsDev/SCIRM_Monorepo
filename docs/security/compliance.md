# Compliance Framework

SCIRM maintains compliance with multiple regulatory frameworks to ensure data security and privacy.

## Supported Frameworks

### SOC 2 Type II
- System and Organization Controls for security, availability, and confidentiality
- Annual third-party audits
- Continuous monitoring and evidence collection

### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- Data subject rights implementation
- Privacy by design architecture
- Data processing agreements and consent management

### HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)
- Protected Health Information (PHI) safeguards
- Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)
- Administrative, physical, and technical safeguards

### FDA 21 CFR Part 11
- Electronic records and electronic signatures
- Audit trails and data integrity
- System validation and qualification

## Compliance Controls

### Data Governance
- Data classification and handling procedures
- Retention and disposal policies
- Cross-border data transfer controls

### Access Management
- Role-based access controls
- Regular access reviews
- Privileged account management

### Audit & Monitoring
- Comprehensive audit logging
- Regular compliance assessments
- Incident response procedures

## Evidence Collection

All compliance evidence is automatically collected and stored in the [Evidence Model](../compliance/evidence-model.md).
